How scoring works
  • Both players show a number from 1 to 6 at the same moment.
  • The batter scores their number. If both numbers match, the batter is out.
  • When the batter is out, sides swap and the second player chases the target.
  • Highest score wins. A toss with both hands decides who picks bat or bowl: an even total goes to the room creator, an odd total to the joiner.
